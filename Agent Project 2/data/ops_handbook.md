# Nimbus Robotics Operations Handbook

This handbook describes how operations work at Nimbus Robotics (a fictional company). It covers travel, equipment, inventory, support tickets and offices.

## Travel and Expenses

### Flights
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 to 10 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 10 hours, or with written approval from a VP.
Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance unless the trip is urgent. Urgent bookings need a one-line justification in the expense report.

### Hotels
The nightly hotel cap depends on the city tier:
- Tier 1 cities (New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco, Zurich): 260 USD per night.
- Tier 2 cities (Berlin, Austin, Toronto, Singapore, Pune): 190 USD per night.
- All other cities: 140 USD per night.
Amounts above the cap are paid by the employee unless the trip owner approves an exception in advance.

### Meals (per diem)
The meal per diem is 65 USD per full travel day and 40 USD for the departure day and the return day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

### Approval thresholds
Trips with an estimated total cost up to 1,500 USD need approval from the employee's manager. Trips above 1,500 USD also need approval from the department head. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of returning.

## Equipment

### Laptop refresh cycle
Every employee laptop is replaced after 36 months of use. Engineers working on simulation or ML training may request a replacement after 24 months. To request a replacement, open a P3 support ticket titled "Laptop refresh" that includes the employee ID and the purchase date of the current laptop.

### Lost or broken equipment
Report lost or stolen devices within 24 hours by opening a P1 ticket. Broken devices that block your work are P2. Accessories (mice, headsets, docks) up to 80 USD can be bought directly and expensed.

## Inventory

### Reorder policy
Every stocked part has a reorder point. When stock falls BELOW the reorder point, purchasing must place an order for enough units to bring the stock up to twice the reorder point (target stock = 2 x reorder point). Orders larger than 5,000 USD in total need approval from the operations lead, Marcus Bell.

### Critical parts
Parts marked as critical (motors, flight controllers and batteries) must never reach zero stock. If a critical part is below its reorder point, purchasing also opens a P2 ticket so the shortage is tracked.

### Supplier lead times
Lead times are listed per part in the inventory system. When a lead time is longer than 21 days, order from the backup supplier if one exists.

## Support Tickets

### Priorities and response times
- P1 (critical, production or security impact): first response within 4 hours, 24/7.
- P2 (high, blocks one person or one team): first response within 1 business day.
- P3 (normal, requests and questions): first response within 3 business days.

### What a good ticket contains
A short title, a description of the problem or request, the requester's employee ID, and the priority. Do not create duplicate tickets: check the open tickets first.

## Offices

### Locations and time zones
- Austin, USA (headquarters): Central Time (UTC-6, UTC-5 in summer). Office hours 08:00 to 18:00.
- Berlin, Germany: Central European Time (UTC+1, UTC+2 in summer). Office hours 08:00 to 18:00.
- Pune, India: India Standard Time (UTC+5:30). Office hours 09:00 to 18:30.

### Visiting another office
Tell the office manager of the host office at least 5 business days before your visit so a desk and a badge can be prepared.

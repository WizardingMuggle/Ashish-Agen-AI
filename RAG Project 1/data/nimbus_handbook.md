## 1. About Nimbus Robotics

Nimbus Robotics was founded in 2014 in Pune, India, by Meera Kulkarni (Chief Executive Officer) and Daniel Osei (Chief Technology Officer). The company started in a rented garage in Baner with a single prototype warehouse robot and three engineers. Today Nimbus employs 340 people across three offices: the headquarters in Pune, an engineering centre in Bengaluru, and a sales and support office in Rotterdam, Netherlands.

Our mission is "to take the heavy, dull and dangerous work off human shoulders". Nimbus builds autonomous robots for warehouses and industrial inspection, and the Helix OS software platform that runs them. Our largest customers are logistics companies, port operators and cold-storage chains.

The company has four core values, known internally as the "four Cs": Curiosity, Candour, Craft and Care. New employees are introduced to these values during the five-day onboarding programme called "Launchpad", which every new hire attends in Pune during their first month.

## 2. Working Hours and Remote Work

### 2.1 Core hours

Nimbus uses flexible working hours. Every employee must be available during the core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time (IST), Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees can organise their day as they wish, as long as they work an average of 40 hours per week. Employees in the Rotterdam office follow core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time.

### 2.2 Hybrid work and anchor days

Nimbus follows a hybrid model. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are "anchor days": all employees are expected in their home office on anchor days so that teams can collaborate in person. Monday and Friday are flexible days that can be worked from home.

Employees who work from home receive a remote work stipend of ₹4,000 per month to cover internet and electricity costs. The stipend is paid automatically with the monthly salary. Employees may also claim a one-time home office allowance of ₹25,000 to buy a desk, chair or monitor; receipts are required.

### 2.3 Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 calendar days per year, subject to manager approval and a check by the HR team for tax and visa issues. Requests must be submitted at least three weeks in advance through the HR portal "Compass".

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at two days per month. Unused annual leave can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days; carried-over days must be used before 31 March, after which they expire. Annual leave requests of more than five consecutive days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

### 3.2 Recharge Week

In addition to annual leave, the whole company shuts down during the last week of December. This is called "Recharge Week". It does not count against an employee's annual leave balance. A small on-call rota from the support team covers Priority 1 customer incidents during Recharge Week; on-call engineers receive two extra days of leave in January as compensation.

### 3.3 Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required only when an employee is sick for more than three consecutive working days. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year. Employees should inform their manager before 10:00 on the first day of sickness.

### 3.4 Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken at any time during the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Parents returning from leave may work a reduced schedule of 30 hours per week for the first two months at full pay.

### 3.5 Other leave

- Bereavement leave: 5 days for the loss of a close family member.
- Marriage leave: 3 days, once during employment at Nimbus.
- Volunteering leave: 2 days per year to volunteer for a registered charity.
- Exam leave: up to 4 days per year for exams related to an approved course.

## 4. Travel and Expenses

### 4.1 Booking travel

All business travel must be booked through the travel portal "Voyager". Flights shorter than six hours are booked in economy class. For flights of six hours or longer, employees may book premium economy. Business class is only allowed for the CEO, the CTO, or with written approval from the CFO, Ananya Rao.

Any single trip with a total estimated cost above ₹50,000 requires approval from a director before booking. Hotel bookings should stay under ₹7,000 per night in India and under €160 per night in Europe.

### 4.2 Per diem

Instead of submitting receipts for every meal, employees receive a daily allowance (per diem):

- Domestic travel within India: ₹3,500 per day.
- International travel: $85 per day (or the equivalent in local currency).

The per diem covers meals, local transport within the city and small incidental expenses. Taxis to and from the airport can be claimed separately.

### 4.3 Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted in Voyager within 30 days of the end of the trip, together with digital copies of all receipts above ₹500. Claims submitted after 30 days need approval from the finance team and may be rejected. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

## 5. Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of ₹60,000. It can be spent on online courses, books, certifications and workshops. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

Each employee may attend one external conference per year, paid by the company, in addition to the learning budget. Employees who attend a conference are asked to give a 20-minute internal talk about what they learned within one month.

On the last Friday of every month, engineers have "Tinker Friday": the whole day can be spent on a self-chosen project, experiment or learning activity. Several Nimbus features started as Tinker Friday projects, including the fleet heat-map in Helix OS.

Nimbus also runs a mentorship programme called "Pair Up". Every employee with less than two years at the company is paired with a senior mentor from a different team. Mentors and mentees meet at least once every two weeks.

## 6. Security and IT

### 6.1 Accounts and passwords

All passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every company system; Nimbus issues every employee a YubiKey hardware security key on their first day. Passwords must never be shared, including with the IT team, who will never ask for them.

### 6.2 Devices

Employees receive a laptop that is refreshed every three years. Engineers can choose between a MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad running Linux. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours so the device can be remotely wiped.

### 6.3 Security incidents

Any suspected security incident — a phishing email, a lost YubiKey, suspicious login alerts — must be reported to security@nimbus.example within 1 hour of discovery. The security team runs a company-wide simulated incident called the "Code Red drill" twice a year, in March and September.

Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage. Robot telemetry logs from customer sites are classified as confidential.

## 7. Products

### 7.1 NX-220 "Atlas" warehouse robot

The NX-220, codenamed "Atlas", is an autonomous mobile robot for moving pallets and shelving units inside warehouses.

- Maximum payload: 220 kg
- Maximum speed: 2.2 metres per second (1.5 m/s when carrying a load above 150 kg)
- Battery life: 8.5 hours of continuous operation
- Charging time: 90 minutes from 10% to 100% on the AutoDock charging station
- Navigation: 360-degree LiDAR plus two stereo depth cameras
- Operating temperature: −10 °C to 45 °C, which makes it suitable for cold-storage warehouses

An Atlas robot automatically returns to its AutoDock when its battery falls below 15%. A standard fleet license allows up to 50 Atlas robots per site; larger fleets need an Enterprise license.

### 7.2 NX-90 "Sparrow" inspection drone

The NX-90, codenamed "Sparrow", is an indoor and outdoor inspection drone used for checking high shelving, roofs, cranes and storage tanks.

- Flight time: 38 minutes per battery
- Maximum wind resistance: 12 metres per second; the drone refuses to take off when sustained wind exceeds this limit
- Ingress protection: IP54 (protected against dust and splashing water, but not heavy rain)
- Camera: 48-megapixel visual camera plus a thermal camera
- Maximum flight altitude: 120 metres above ground level

Every Sparrow is sold with three batteries and a hot-swap charging case. A Sparrow pilot must complete the internal "Sky Basics" certification before flying at a customer site.

### 7.3 Helix OS

Helix OS is the software platform that runs every Nimbus robot and drone. The current stable release is version 4.7.2. Helix OS includes the Fleet Manager dashboard, which shows every robot on a live map, plans routes, and assigns tasks. Updates are released every six weeks and are installed over the air during a maintenance window chosen by the customer.

## 8. Troubleshooting and Error Codes

Error codes starting with "E" are errors that stop the robot. Codes starting with "W" are warnings; the robot keeps working.

### E-4471: LiDAR calibration drift

The LiDAR sensor's measurements no longer match the expected map. The robot slows to 0.5 m/s and stops after 3 minutes. Fix: run the command `helix calibrate --lidar` from the Fleet Manager console. If the error returns within 24 hours after calibration, the sensor mounting bracket is probably loose or bent and must be replaced by a certified technician (part number BRK-LD-07).

### E-1032: Battery thermal warning

The battery temperature has exceeded 55 °C. The robot stops immediately and parks. Fix: let the robot cool down for at least 20 minutes, then check that the cooling fan at the rear is not blocked by dust or packaging material. If E-1032 appears more than twice in one week, open a Priority 2 support ticket.

### E-2209: Lost connection to Fleet Manager

The robot has lost its Wi-Fi connection to the Fleet Manager. After 45 seconds without connection, the robot enters "safe-park" mode: it moves to the nearest safe spot at the side of the aisle and waits. It reconnects automatically when the network returns. Persistent E-2209 errors usually point to Wi-Fi dead zones; Nimbus recommends a site survey.

### W-0815: Payload imbalance

The load on the robot is unevenly distributed. The robot continues working but reduces its speed by 30% and avoids sharp turns. Fix: re-centre the load on the next stop.

## 9. Customer Support and SLAs

Customer issues are classified into three priority levels, with the following first-response times (Service Level Agreements):

- Priority 1 (P1) — a robot fleet is down or there is a safety risk: response within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Priority 2 (P2) — a single robot is down or performance is degraded: response within 4 business hours.
- Priority 3 (P3) — questions, feature requests and minor bugs: response within 2 business days.

Every P1 incident is assigned a "Duty Engineer" who stays responsible until the incident is resolved. After every P1 incident, the support team writes a blameless post-mortem within 5 business days and shares it with the customer.

## 10. Performance Reviews and Compensation

Nimbus holds performance reviews twice a year, in April and October. The review framework is called "Growth Compass" and uses a rating scale from 1 (below expectations) to 5 (exceptional). Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from at least three peers, and the manager's assessment.

Employees can earn an annual bonus of up to 15% of their base salary, based on company results and the employee's Growth Compass ratings. Salary increases are applied in the April cycle only. Promotions can happen in either cycle.

Nimbus also offers an employee stock option plan (ESOP). Options vest over four years with a one-year cliff: 25% vests after the first year, and the remainder vests monthly over the following three years.

**Is there free food?**
All offices serve a free lunch on anchor days (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday). Snacks and coffee are available every day.

**Who do I contact about payroll?**
Write to payroll@nimbus.example. Payroll questions are answered within 2 business days. Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.

**What happens if I lose my YubiKey?**
Report it to security@nimbus.example within 1 hour (it is a security incident), then collect a replacement from the IT desk. Your old key is revoked remotely.
